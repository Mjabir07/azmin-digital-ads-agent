# AZMIN DIGITAL — brand rulebook

## Who we are
- **AZMIN DIGITAL** — digital agency in Dubai for growing businesses (UAE first, KSA later).
- Website: https://azmindigital.com · Instagram: @azmin_digital · Facebook Page: "Azmin Digital".
- Positioning: *one team for websites, AI automation, marketing and business email — built to bring you customers.*
- Website headline (may be quoted): "Websites, automation and growth, built to work."

## Services (only these — never invent others)
1. **Website Design** — fast, mobile-first, SEO-ready, analytics from day one.
2. **AI Automation** — WhatsApp & website chatbots, workflow automation, lead capture and follow-up.
3. **Digital Marketing** — Google & Meta ads, SEO, social content, monthly reports.
4. **Business Email Setup** — Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Zoho; MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC; migration.
Also offered (mention lightly): apps, web hosting & cloud servers.

## Approved claims (source: config/packages.yaml — re-read it; it wins if different)
- Starter Combo website: **AED 999 incl. domain + 1 year hosting** (use in ads/offer posts, NOT in profile bios).
- Delivery: **"Ready in as little as 1 day"** — never "guaranteed 24 hours".
- Process promises from the website: clear quote before we start, weekly progress, support after launch.
- Page count, renewal price, custom quotes → never state; "Jabir will confirm".

## Never
- Invented prices, discounts or deadlines; guaranteed results ("100% more sales"); fake reviews/testimonials.
- Client work without permission. Approved portfolio: **msjtechnicaluae.com** and **purepreciousjewellery.com** (both shown on azmindigital.com). Grace IT: do NOT use.
- Personal-attribute targeting language ("Are you struggling with money?") — Meta policy.
- Arabic copy published without a native-speaker review (flag it instead).
- Competitor names or negative comparisons.

## Voice
- Confident, clear, helpful; short sentences; plain English (many readers use English as a second language).
- Benefit first, then proof, then one call to action (usually "Chat with us on WhatsApp").
- Emojis: max 1 per line, purposeful (🌐 🤖 📈 📧 ⚡ ✅ 👇). No slang, no hype words ("revolutionary", "best in the world").

## Visual system
- Colours: blue `#0062F5`, cyan `#00D2E2`, navy `#071A3D`, ink `#0B1B3A`, light bg `#F4F8FF`, WhatsApp green `#25D366` (CTA only).
- Fonts: Poppins (EN), Cairo (AR) — files in `brand/fonts/`.
- Logo: `brand/logo.png` (flat blue AZ). On dark backgrounds put it on a white rounded tile. Never the old 3D logo.
- Look: dark navy gradients + angled translucent bars echoing the logo strokes; generous spacing; one idea per design.
- Reels: hook in first 1.5 s, burned-in captions, 9:16 with Stories/Reels safe zones (top 250 px, bottom 340 px clear),
  premium motion (kinetic type, device mockups, smooth easing) — never basic slideshow fades.
