# WhatsApp sales (from ad chat to paid website)

Load `azmin-brand` first. Prices only from `config/packages.yaml` (today: Starter Combo from AED 999 incl. domain +
1 year hosting, ready in as little as 1 day). Page count, renewal price, custom quotes → Jabir confirms personally.
Never invent testimonials, discounts or deadlines. The copy-paste version for Jabir is
`marketing/sales/whatsapp-scripts.md`; keep both in sync. Sales ideas adapted from the MIT-licensed
ethanplusai/harvey playbooks (lead_qualification, objection_handling, offer_strategy), rewritten for UAE WhatsApp.

## Principles
- **Speed wins:** reply within 5 minutes during business hours; the first agency to reply usually gets the job.
- **Ask before you pitch:** 2–3 questions, one per message, then recommend ONE option. No price dumps, no walls of text.
- **Short messages, plain English** (many owners use English as a second language). Mirror Arabic only if they write
  in Arabic and Jabir can handle it; never auto-translate prices or terms.
- **Proof = real work only:** msjtechnicaluae.com. No fake reviews.
- **Handover to Jabir (AI assistant rule):** call request, custom quote, complaint/upset, or anything not covered.

## Ice-breaker buttons → first reply (ad: "AZD | UAE | WhatsApp Leads | Website Design")
| Button tapped | Intent | First move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I have a business – need a website | High | Ask business + activity, then goal + timing |
| Starting a new business | High (needs trust) | Congratulate, ask activity + launch date, mention domain/email setup |
| Redesign my current website | High (has budget history) | Ask for current site link + what's not working |
| Just checking prices | Low/unknown | Give the Starter Combo facts + one question to re-qualify |

## Qualifying questions (one at a time)
1. Business name and what you do? (and the current website/Instagram link if any)
2. Main goal: more enquiries/calls, online booking/sales, or a professional image?
3. When do you need it live? 4. (Only after value is clear) Budget range, if they ask about bigger sites.

## Lead score (1–10) — tag every chat
| Factor | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Real operating business (trade licence/shop/active Instagram) or launching within 30 days | 0–3 |
| Clear need (no site / bad site / wants enquiries) | 0–3 |
| Timing: needs it within 2 weeks = 2, this month = 1, "someday" = 0 | 0–2 |
| Decision-maker (owner/partner) | 0–1 |
| Engaged (answers questions, asks about process) | 0–1 |
**8–10 Hot:** same-day quote, offer a 10-min call. **5–7 Warm:** answer + follow-up D1/D3/D7.
**1–4 Cold:** polite info, one follow-up, then close. **Disqualify:** job seekers, resellers wanting free work,
students, spam, or asking for services we don't offer.

## Reply-intent tags (for the leads sheet and the daily report)
`interested` · `needs_info` · `price_objection` · `needs_partner` · `not_now` · `has_provider` · `not_a_fit` ·
`unsubscribe` (stop messaging) · `upset` (Jabir handles personally, today).

## Objections (Listen → Acknowledge → Ask → Respond → Confirm)
- **Too expensive:** acknowledge; anchor on what's included (domain + hosting + mobile + WhatsApp button, ready in
  as little as 1 day); ask what budget they had in mind; Starter is already the entry option, don't discount.
- **Need to ask my partner:** offer a short summary they can forward; ask when they'll decide; follow up then.
- **Not now / later:** ask when; promise a reminder; set the follow-up date.
- **Fiverr/freelancer is cheaper:** local Dubai team, same-day WhatsApp support, domain + hosting handled, support
  after launch. Never insult competitors.
- **Send me details:** send the short offer card + ask one qualifying question so the chat continues.
- **I already have someone:** ask what they'd improve; offer a free quick website check; stay friendly.

## Offer ladder (never pitch the top first)
Free quick website check → **Starter Combo (AED 999)** → custom/bigger website (Jabir quotes) → add-ons:
business email setup, WhatsApp AI chatbot, Google Business Profile, ads management. Cross-sell only after the
website is agreed or delivered.

## Close
Recommend one package → confirm scope in 3 bullets → send quote (PDF) → 50% advance to start → share timeline.
Ask for referral and a Google review only after a happy delivery (never pay for or fake reviews).

## Follow-ups
Day 1 (useful nudge) · Day 3 (show the MSJ site / an idea for their business) · Day 7 ("Shall I keep your slot or
close the file for now?"). Stop after Day 7 or on `unsubscribe`.
