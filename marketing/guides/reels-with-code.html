1 Start a Remotion project

$ npx create-video@latest

Then give Claude the official Remotion know-how:

$ npx skills add remotion-dev/skills

2 Ask Claude for the reel

Open Claude Code in the project and describe it like a brief:

3 Add a free voice

$ pip install kokoro-onnx soundfile

Write one line per scene, generate a WAV per line, and let Claude time the captions to the words.

4 Render

$ npx remotion render src/index.ts MyReel out/reel.mp4

Captions, music and sound effects are all in the code, so one command makes the finished MP4.

Why code instead of an editor?

  • Reuse: one template, a new episode every day. Change the words, keep the look.
  • On brand every time: colours, fonts and logo live in one file.
  • Fast changes: "make it slower", "new colour" or "bigger captions" is one sentence to Claude.

Tips from our own reels

  • Keep it 40–50 seconds, one idea per screen, voice at normal speed.
  • Captions: 3 words at a time, big, the spoken word highlighted.
  • Music low under the voice; small sound effects on each new screen.
  • Make your own sound effects with code, or use a licensed library: no copyright strikes.

Be honest about AI

Yara is an AI presenter and we say so. Label AI people and voices clearly, and never present them as real customers.
