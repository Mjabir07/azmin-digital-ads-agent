# WhatsApp reply scripts: copy, paste, adjust the name

For the Meta ad chats. Keep messages short, send one question at a time, and reply within 5 minutes if you can.
Prices: Starter Combo **from AED 999** (business website + free domain + 1 year hosting, ready in as little as 1 day).
Bigger or custom websites: "I'll prepare a quote for you". Never promise page counts or renewal prices in chat.

---

## 1. First reply, by the button they tapped

**"I have a business – need a website"**
> Hi! 👋 Thanks for reaching out, I'm Jabir from AZMIN DIGITAL. Happy to help.
> Quick question first: what's your business name and what do you do?

**"Starting a new business"**
> Congratulations on the new business! 🎉 I'm Jabir from AZMIN DIGITAL.
> What will the business do, and when are you planning to launch?

**"Redesign my current website"**
> Hi! 👋 I'm Jabir from AZMIN DIGITAL. Can you share your current website link?
> And what's the main thing you're not happy with: design, speed, not getting enquiries?

**"Just checking prices"**
> Hi! 👋 Our Starter Combo is **from AED 999**: a professional business website, free domain and 1 year hosting,
> mobile-friendly with a WhatsApp button. Ready in as little as 1 day.
> What kind of business is it for? Then I can tell you exactly what fits.

## 2. Next questions (one per message)
> What's the main goal: more calls/enquiries, online booking or sales, or a professional image?

> When do you need it live?

## 3. Recommend + proof
> Based on what you said, our **Starter Combo** fits well: [3 bullets: e.g. home, services, contact + WhatsApp button].
> Here's a recent one we built: msjtechnicaluae.com
> Shall I send you the quote?

For a bigger site: *"For this I'll prepare a custom quote. Can we do a quick 10-minute call today or tomorrow?"*

## 4. Close
> Great! To start we take a 50% advance and the rest at launch. I'll send the quote now.
> Once the advance is in, we can have the first version ready in as little as 1 day.

## 5. Objections
**"Too expensive"**
> I understand. AED 999 already includes the domain, 1 year hosting, mobile design and the WhatsApp button, with no
> extra costs to get online. What budget did you have in mind? Then I can suggest the best option.

**"I need to ask my partner"**
> Of course! Shall I send a short summary you can forward to them? When do you think you'll decide?

**"Maybe later / not now"**
> No problem. When would be a good time? I'll send you a reminder then.

**"Someone on Fiverr is cheaper"**
> That's fair. The difference with us: we're here in Dubai, you can WhatsApp us any time, and we handle the domain,
> hosting and support after launch. Happy to show you what we've built so you can compare.

**"Send me the details"**
> Sure! [paste Starter Combo text from 1. "Just checking prices"]
> What's your business, so I can tell you what fits best?

**"I already have a web designer"**
> Great that you're sorted! If you'd ever like a second opinion, I can do a free quick check of your website. Just
> send the link any time.

## 6. Follow-ups (if they go quiet)
- **Day 1:** *"Hi [name], just checking: did you get a chance to look at the quote? Any questions?"*
- **Day 3:** *"Hi [name], I had an idea for your website: [one idea, e.g. a booking button / Google Maps]. Here's a
  similar site we built: msjtechnicaluae.com"*
- **Day 7:** *"Hi [name], shall I keep your slot this week or close the file for now? No pressure either way 🙂"*

Stop after Day 7, or straight away if they say stop.

## 7. Quick lead score: tag each chat in your notes
- **Hot (8–10):** real business, clear need, wants it within 2 weeks, owner. Send the quote today and offer a call.
- **Warm (5–7):** interested but no rush. Answer, then follow up Day 1/3/7.
- **Cold (1–4):** just browsing. Polite info, one follow-up.
- **Not a fit:** job seekers, students, spam, services we don't do. Reply politely once, then stop.

Note per lead: Date · Name · Business · Button tapped · Score · Status · Quote (AED) · Won (AED).
