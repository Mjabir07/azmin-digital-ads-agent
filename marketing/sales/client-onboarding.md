# New client onboarding: what to ask for (website projects)

Ask only for what the website needs. Never ask for passport or Emirates ID copies, and never ask for passwords in
chat; for logins, ask them to add you as a user or share them on a call.

## Step 1: confirm the deal
- Quote accepted + **50% advance** paid (invoice/receipt sent)
- Agreed scope in writing: package, pages/sections, launch target date

## Step 2: the checklist

**A. Business basics**
1. Business name, exactly as it should appear (and as on the trade licence)
2. What you do in 2–3 lines + the main services/products
3. Contact details for the site: phone, WhatsApp number, email, address, Google Maps link, opening hours
4. Social media links (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn)

**B. Brand**
5. Logo: best quality you have (PNG with transparent background, or AI/SVG/PDF file). If none, tell us
6. Brand colours (if any) and any style you like or dislike
7. 2–3 websites you like (any industry), so we know your taste

**C. Content**
8. Photos: shop/office, team, products or past projects (originals from phone are fine; more is better)
9. Services/products list: name + 1–2 lines each (+ prices only if you want them shown)
10. "About us" points: when you started, what makes you different, areas you serve (we write the text)
11. Customer reviews you are happy to show (screenshots from Google/WhatsApp, with permission)

**D. Domain & accounts**
12. Domain: 2–3 name ideas (we check availability), or, if you already own one, where it is registered
    (we'll ask you to add us or share access on a call; never send passwords in chat)
13. Existing website/hosting (redesigns only): link + who manages it now
14. Google Business Profile: do you have one? (we can set it up / add us as manager)
15. Business email wanted? (e.g. info@yourbusiness.com, via Google Workspace / Microsoft 365 / Zoho; quoted separately)

Note: a **.ae** domain needs a UAE trade licence or trademark (we register it in the business name);
.com has no such requirement.

## Step 3: timeline
Content received → first version in as little as 1 day → client feedback (1 round included) → final 50% → launch →
support after launch.
