# AZMIN DIGITAL brand ambassador — "Yara"

An AI-generated presenter (made in Google Flow) who speaks for AZMIN DIGITAL in every reel.
Same face, outfit style and voice every time, so people recognise the brand.

## Who she is
- **Name:** Yara (only used inside our team / Flow; on screen she just presents — never claims to be a
  real employee, customer or reviewer).
- **Look:** young Arab woman, looks 20–22, small slim delicate face (soft V-shaped jawline, small nose), smooth youthful skin, bright youthful face, big warm smile, fresh dewy make-up, soft-brown eyes.
- **Style (modest, modern, trendy):** loosely draped satin hijab in a soft modern colour (sky blue, blush or
  cream), oversized cropped cream or light-blue blazer over a fitted white top, delicate gold layered necklace,
  smartwatch, no logos on clothes. Feels like a young Dubai creator / tech founder, not a corporate banker.
- **Settings (rotate):** bright modern Dubai office with glass walls and soft daylight; a stylish café;
  a creative studio desk with a laptop. Always clean, premium, softly lit.
- **Voice:** calm, confident, friendly; clear international English with a light Arabic accent;
  medium pace; never shouting or "salesy".
- **Body language:** looks straight into camera, natural hand gestures, holds a phone/laptop when
  showing work, points down when the WhatsApp number appears.

## Rules
- Speaks ONLY scripts we write (prices only from `config/packages.yaml`).
- Never presented as a client testimonial or a real person giving a review.
- Respectful, modest framing: waist-up shots, no sudden glamour/beauty-ad looks.
- Same description pasted into every prompt so the voice and look stay consistent.

"Yara speaks clearly in English to camera with a calm, confident, friendly voice and a light Arabic accent,
