# Market research — UAE small-business websites (October 2026)

Prepared by the agent for the launch campaign. Sources are vendor pages and blogs found by web
search, so treat prices as **indicative**, not verified. Refresh monthly.

## 1. What competitors charge (Dubai)

| Offer seen | Price | Domain + hosting? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Web Makers — 5-page static starter | AED 999 | **Not included** (extra) | WordPress 6 pages AED 2,500; stores from AED 3,999; care plan AED 299/month |
| Innomedia — basic package | from AED 1,200 | **Included, 1 year** + logo | Mobile-friendly, WhatsApp integration |
| Budget freelancers / small studios | from ~AED 299–399 | Usually unclear | Quality and support uncertain |
| "Typical" agency brochure site (guides) | AED 2,900–9,500; 5-page site AED 5,000–8,000 | Varies | Most guides treat domain/hosting as yearly extras |

Yearly running costs quoted in guides: domain AED 100–300, hosting AED 500–3,000.
A `.ae` domain needs a UAE trade licence.

## 2. What this means for AZMIN DIGITAL

- **AED 999 with domain + 1 year hosting free is at the low end of the professional market.**
  A same-price rival charges extra for domain and hosting, which can cost several hundred AED a year.
  **Lead with this in the ads:** "AED 999, domain + hosting included."
- Be clear about **what happens in year 2** (the renewal price). Guides warn buyers about hidden renewal
  costs, so being upfront about it helps build trust. → *Jabir to confirm the renewal price.*
- The cheaper freelancers (AED 299–399) compete on price alone. Beat them on **local team, Arabic +
  English, after-launch support, 7-day delivery**. This matches the playbook's answer to the "Fiverr" objection.

## 3. What small-business owners struggle with

Older but still useful findings (no 2026 survey found):
- A review of ~11,800 UAE SME websites found many had **no contact form or phone number that was easy to find**,
  ~90% had **no social media links**, ~80% had **no local address**, and many **didn't work well on phones**.
  → Good angles for the *Free website audit* and *Pain* ads.
- About half of UAE businesses used an outside professional to build their site, and nearly half planned to
  **grow their online presence and online sales**. → There is real demand.
- **Online marketing and branding** is a top challenge for SMEs. → Mention Google Business setup and the
  WhatsApp button as practical benefits.

## 4. Meta ad rules to respect (affects copy)

- **No "personal attributes":** don't write copy that says or hints at who the viewer is (for example
  "Are you struggling with money?"). Talk about the **product's benefits** instead.
  Asking "Starting a new business?" is about a situation, not a protected attribute, but keep it neutral.
- **No guaranteed results.** Avoid "guaranteed more customers" or "100% more sales". "Live in 7 days" is fine
  only if we always deliver in 7 days.
- Prices in ads must match what we actually charge (AED 999 combo).
