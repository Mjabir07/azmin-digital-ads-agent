# AZMIN DIGITAL — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

AZMIN DIGITAL ("we", "us") is a digital agency based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates (azmindigital.com).
This policy explains what personal data we collect when you contact us through our website, our Facebook and
Instagram pages, our ads or WhatsApp, and how we use it.

## What we collect
- **Contact details you give us:** your name, phone/WhatsApp number, email address and business name.
- **Your messages:** what you write to us on WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram or email.
- **Basic ad data:** when you reply to one of our Facebook/Instagram ads, Meta tells us which ad you came from.
  We receive aggregated ad statistics (reach, clicks, cost) from Meta, not your personal profile.

## How we use it
- To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and deliver the services you ask for.
- To follow up about your enquiry (you can tell us to stop at any time).
- To measure which of our ads and posts work, using aggregated statistics.

We do **not** sell your data, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your enquiry.

## Who we share it with
Only the services we use to run the business: Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp), Google (Workspace and
Sheets for our lead list), GitHub (our internal automation), Telegram (internal notifications) and Anthropic
(AI tools that help us draft replies and reports). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

## How long we keep it
We keep enquiry details while we're in contact with you and for up to 24 months after, unless you ask us to
delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer (for example invoices).

## Your rights and data deletion
You can ask us at any time to see, correct or **delete** the personal data we hold about you.
Send a WhatsApp message to **+971 56 724 7597** or use the contact form on **azmindigital.com**, saying
"Delete my data". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.
